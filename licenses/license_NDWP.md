# General information

Data from India's National Water Data Portal (NWDP) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in GROW-Qual was retrieved from the NWDP website (https://nwdp.nwic.gov.in/). The national water data policy, including licensing conditions, can be found here: https://nwdp.nwic.gov.in/pdf/national-water-data-policy.pdf
